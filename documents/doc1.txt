VALORANT Champions Tour (VCT) is Riot Games' global esports circuit for
VALORANT, organized into four international leagues: Americas, EMEA,
Pacific, and China. Each season begins with a Kickoff event, followed by
two stages of regular season play, and culminates in two Masters
tournaments and a season-ending Champions tournament. For the 2026 season,
Riot introduced a triple-elimination Kickoff format with twelve teams per
region, each guaranteed three opportunities before elimination. The top
three teams from each region's Kickoff advance to the first Masters event
of the year, held in Santiago, Chile. The season concludes with VALORANT
Champions 2026 in Shanghai, China, where sixteen teams, four from each
international league, compete in a group stage followed by a
double-elimination playoff bracket, with the grand final played as a
best-of-five.
