VALORANT Champions 2025 was held in Paris, France, from September 12 to
October 5, 2025, at the Accor Arena, with a total prize pool of
$2,250,000. Sixteen teams competed in a double-elimination format. NRG
Esports won the tournament, defeating Fnatic 3-2 in the grand final after
a tense back-and-forth series. The win marked North America's return to
the top of international VALORANT, after a period of European and EMEA
dominance. NRG's rookie player Brock "brawk" Somerhalder was named
tournament MVP, finishing with a strong kill-to-death ratio. Fnatic
reached the grand final after defeating DRX in the lower bracket final,
but fell short of the title, finishing as runner-up at three major
international events across 2025: Masters Toronto, the Esports World Cup,
and VALORANT Champions.
