NRG Esports is a VALORANT team based in North America, competing in the
VCT Americas league. NRG won VALORANT Champions 2025 in Paris, defeating
Fnatic in the grand final. The team's in-game leader, Ethan "Ethan"
Arnold, became the first player to win VALORANT Champions twice, having
previously won the title with Evil Geniuses in 2023. Other members of
NRG's 2025 championship roster included Sam "s0m" Oh, Pujan "FNS" Mehta,
Adam "mada" Pampuch, and Andrew "Verno" Maust, coached by Malkolm "bonkar"
Rench. After the Paris victory, veteran player s0m announced he would step
away from competitive play. Heading into the 2026 season, NRG's roster is
built around Ethan, along with Georgio "keiko" Sanassy, Adam "mada"
Pampuch, Logan "skuba" Jenkins, and Brock "brawk" Somerhalder.
