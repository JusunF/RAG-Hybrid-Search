Fnatic is a British VALORANT organization competing in the EMEA region.
The team reached the grand final of three major international events in
2025, Masters Toronto, the Esports World Cup, and VALORANT Champions, but
finished as runner-up in all three, most notably losing 3-2 to NRG in the
VALORANT Champions 2025 grand final in Paris. Fnatic's long-time in-game
leader Jake "Boaster" Howlett has been with the organization since 2021
and re-signed for the 2026 season. Star player Emir "Alfajer" Beder missed
part of the Champions 2025 playoffs due to a health issue but returned for
the grand final. Fnatic's roster heading into 2026 includes Boaster,
Alfajer, Austin "crashies" Roberts, Kajetan "kaajak" Haremski, and French
newcomer Sylvain "Veqaj" Pattyn, replacing former player Timofey
"Chronicle" Khromov.
