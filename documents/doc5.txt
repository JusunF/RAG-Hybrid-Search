Paper Rex, commonly abbreviated as PRX, is a VALORANT organization based in Singapore, competing in the
Pacific region and known for an aggressive, unconventional playstyle. The
team won VCT 2025 Masters Toronto, their first international title after
multiple near-misses, including grand final losses at Masters Copenhagen
and VALORANT Champions 2023. Paper Rex finished 4th at VALORANT Champions
2025 in Paris. The team's long-standing core includes Jason "f0rsakeN"
Susanto, who transitioned from duelist to in-game leader and controller
player, and Wang "Jinggg" Jing Jie, widely regarded as one of the best
duelists in the world. Other roster members have included Ilya "something"
Petrov, Ahmad Khalish "d4v41" Rusyaidee, and, more recently, Adrian "invy"
Reyes.
