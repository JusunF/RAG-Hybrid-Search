Several notable player transfers shaped the VALORANT esports scene around
the 2025 season. Erick "aspas" Santos, long regarded as one of the best
duelists in the world and previously with Leviatan, signed with Brazilian
organization MIBR. Max "Demon1" Mazanov, a 2023 VALORANT Champions winner
with Evil Geniuses, was signed by Leviatan to help fill the void left by
aspas's departure. Nikita "Derke" Sirmitev, a former Fnatic star, moved to
Team Vitality alongside former Fnatic teammate Timofey "Chronicle"
Khromov. These moves were part of the broader "rostermania" period that
follows each VALORANT Champions tournament, when teams reassess their
lineups heading into the next season.
